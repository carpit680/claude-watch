Set up in three steps.

About five minutes. You'll need a Mac with Claude Code and an Apple Watch.

1

Get the app on your watch

Install Wrist Bridge from the App Store on your Apple Watch, and open it.

2

Run one command on your Mac

It installs the bridge to start at login, and signs it in to Claude if needed. Then it shows a 6-digit pairing code.

$ npx wrist-bridge setup
3

Pair

On the watch, tap Pair with your Mac and enter the code. Both screens then show a check number. If they match, type y on the Mac. That's it.

You'll need: macOS with Claude Code signed in · Node 22 or later (brew install node) · Apple Watch on watchOS 26 or later.
Tip: keep your Mac plugged in. On battery, or with the lid closed, it sleeps, and the watch can't reach it.

How it works.

Three small pieces, all open source.

The bridge

A small program on your Mac. It reads your Claude Code sessions, passes your messages to them, and notices when one needs you. It connects out to the relay, and accepts no incoming connections.

The relay

Connects your watch to your Mac without either one needing a public address. It can't read what passes through, and it delivers your alerts. You can run your own.

Your code stays yours.

Wrist Bridge collects nothing. The details:

Encrypted end to end

The watch and Mac agree on a key when they pair (X25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305). Messages, replies and session lists are sealed with it.

Pairing you confirm

A one-time code, plus a check number you compare on both screens. That stops anyone in between, the relay included, from swapping keys.

One honest exception

Alert text, such as a session's title and its question, passes through the relay to Apple's push service. Apple needs it readable to display the alert. It isn't stored.

Full details in the privacy policy and security notes.

Questions.

The short answers.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app, the bridge and the relay are free. Your sessions use your own Claude plan, through Claude Code on your Mac.

Can I approve permission prompts from the watch?

Not yet: Claude Code only lets you approve them at the Mac. The watch tells you one is waiting. Questions and choices from Claude can be answered on the watch.

Will it work with sessions I have open in the Claude desktop app?

Yes. Your message is delivered into the live session and it answers there, so you see the conversation on your Mac too. Sessions that aren't open anywhere are resumed in the background.

The watch says my Mac isn't connected.

Your Mac is probably asleep. Keep it plugged in. To check the bridge, run wrist-bridge status.

How do I remove it?

Run wrist-bridge unpair to cut off your watch, and wrist-bridge uninstall to remove the bridge. Then delete the app from your watch.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. Wrist Bridge is an independent, open-source project. Claude and Claude Code are trademarks of Anthropic.
